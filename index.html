How my tools work

Duplicate payment detector, step by step

A simplified version of the kind of payment review I did at the Port Authority
  1. Each line is read as vendor, invoice, and amount.
  2. A key is built from all three. If the key was already seen, it is an exact duplicate and its amount counts as money at risk.
  3. If only the vendor and amount match but the invoice differs, it is flagged as a possible duplicate for a person to review, because vendors can legitimately bill the same amount twice.
  4. The tool reports the counts and the total at risk.

Next improvements: upload a CSV file, add a date window, and show results in a sortable table.

Serve tracker and hold calculator

Tennis analytics

The tracker counts five outcomes per service point and turns them into first serve percentage, points won on each serve, and double fault rate. The hold calculator uses the share of serve points won to estimate the chance of winning a service game, assuming points are independent. Real matches have pressure and momentum, so a next step is to test that assumption with real match data.
